# 🧭 SE Navigator

> An interactive visualization of the Systems Engineering lifecycle.

**SE Navigator** is a lightweight, browser-based tool that maps out the systems engineering journey—from initial problem discovery to final deployment. It provides engineers, product managers, and architects with actionable context, industry standards, and ready-to-use LLM/Agent prompts for every phase of development.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Interactive Lifecycle Graph:** Visualize the flow and dependencies of systems engineering phases.
* **Phase Context Panels:** Click any node to reveal its purpose, real-world examples, and relevant ISO/INCOSE standards.
* **Built-in LLM Prompts:** Copy phase-specific roleplay prompts designed to bootstrap your work with AI agents.
* **Local Storage Notes:** Jot down decisions and observations directly in the app; they save automatically to your browser.
* **Phase Filtering:** Isolate specific stages of the lifecycle (Discovery, Planning, Design, Build, Testing, Delivery) for focused viewing.
* **JSON Export:** Download your entire framework state, including your custom notes, for documentation or sharing.
